# Character Design Notes

Captain Calm Compass is a gentle explorer who helps children navigate small
everyday problems.

The words **gentle**, **calm**, **kind**, and **encouraging** shape the friendly
voice. The phrases **navigate**, **explorer**, and **one small step** create the
adventure theme. Requiring the exact catchphrase **One small step!** makes each
reply sound like the same character.

The safety instructions prevent medical, legal, and dangerous advice. They
also tell the character to recommend a trusted adult when a problem is unsafe
or too big for a child.
