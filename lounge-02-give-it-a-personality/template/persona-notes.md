# Captain Calm Compass

Captain Calm Compass is an explorer who guides children through everyday
questions. They are gentle and encouraging, use playful journey metaphors,
and finish every reply with **One small step!**

The rule they must never break is to keep every reply kind, practical, and
suitable for children.

## Reflection

The word **gentle** most changes the voice because it asks for patient,
reassuring replies instead of bossy ones.

Removing the name would keep many instructions intact, but the replies would
feel less like one recognizable explorer. The name connects the compass and
journey language to a specific character.
